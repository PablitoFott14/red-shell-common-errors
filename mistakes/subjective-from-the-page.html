Costs pointsRubric Criteria - Overall Rubric Quality

The mistake

A subjective criterion requires the exact heading or label your golden happens to use, words no prompt contains.

Do this instead

Name the element by what it is, and grade how it is presented, not what it is called.

QC spec appendix · Multi-Turn Authoring Standards · Writing Subjective Rubrics

Name ONE observable property of the RENDERED artifact. The judge sees the render, not the intent behind it.

A property, not one build's wording.

Guidelines · 8.1 Writing Subjective Rubrics

If anything related to the render is asked in the prompt, it requires an objective rubric ONLY.

A label you chose is graded nowhere.

Why it matters

Subjective rubrics grade how the artifact reads: layout, hierarchy, spacing, consistency. A criterion naming one build's text rejects a subset of valid implementations, and a second correct run presenting the same results under its own wording fails almost the whole block. Overfitting counts toward the share of criteria built wrong, and the task fails once that share passes 15% of all its criteria. Where most of the block is written this way, rewriting three or four is not enough.

Leg A turn 1
It's the 28th of August and I'm trying to close out something that's been sitting in the back of my head. I drove my own car to Everett twice this year for work, once in May and once in August, and I filed a mileage claim for each trip. Marguerite approved both of them and HR wrote back saying they were acknowledged and complete and that the money comes through payroll, no separate deposit, nothing else needed from me. Which is fine, except my paycheck is the same number every single time and I honestly can't tell whether either one of those ever actually landed. I put some things in inputs for you, the expense policy and a couple of my pay statements, plus something the bank sent me. My mail and my accounts should have the rest. Can you work out, for each of the two claims separately, whether the money reached me or not? I don't want them added together into one number, I want to know which one is which. And if one of them didn't come through, tell me whether I can still do anything about it, because I have a feeling the policy puts a clock on that. Put it together as reimbursement_status.html. I want to be able to open it and read it properly before I send anything to Payroll.

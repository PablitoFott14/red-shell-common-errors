Stopping rules that do not gate the turn

costs points
Milestones - Continuation Criteria & Assets
1

What the issue is

Two opposite failures of the Continuation criteria field. Either its clauses cover only part of what the turn asked, so the conversation moves on with a requirement never checked, or they require something the turn has no way to produce, such as a document that only arrives at a later turn, so a correct run is held at that turn.

2

Why it matters, and what the project expects

Continuation criteria are what the simulator advances on. A missing clause means every later turn builds on a hole. A clause the turn cannot satisfy does the opposite: it holds a correct run at that turn, on a condition the user never created.

Attempter Guidelines6.2 Writing the Milestones
Continuation criteria: This is the stopping rule for a turn, and they are read by you, never typed to the agent. Write one clause per requirement of that turn, separated by pipes, each of them true or false on inspection. Continuation criteria should remain at the intent level, just like the milestone itself.

One clause for each requirement of that turn: none left out, none borrowed from a later turn, each decidable by looking.

QC specMilestones - Continuation Criteria & Assets
Continuation criteria are present and checkable but under-specified — they would let the simulator advance while a requirement of that turn is still unmet.

A turn whose clauses skip one of its asks lets the run move on with that ask undone.

3

How to recognise it in your own task

Two questions per row. Would this let the simulator move on while something the turn asked for is still undone? And can this turn actually produce what the clause demands, with nothing from later turns in hand?

4

Real tasks where this happened

Two real tasks in this project are carrying it. They are evidence that it is real and something to practise spotting it on, not a second version of the rule. Open one and it expands here, with the point on the left and the task's own fields on the right. Escape closes it.

5

What to do instead

After writing a turn's clauses, read back that turn's prompt and tick off each ask against a clause. Every ask needs one, each has to be true or false on inspection, and none may name the answer. Any clause that needs a file or an ask from a later turn moves to that later turn's milestone.

The issue, in this task
The setup
Prompt 1 asks for a page built from the monthly exports: what ran, what it was about, which chart types were used, and which pieces still carry a correction, with each piece marked read or confirmed and every count clicking through to its files. Turn 1 carries six continuation clauses.
Where the issue shows up here
The six clauses cover the file, the cards, type and subject, click through, and read versus confirmed. None of them mentions corrections.
Why that is wrong
As written, the simulator advances to turn 2 on a page that never mentions a correction at all: one of the opening asks, silently dropped.
What it should have said
Add one clause at the same intent level as the others: Pieces carrying a correction are marked as such on their own card.

Milestones - Continuation Criteria & Assets

What the mistake is

Two opposite failures of the same field. Either the clauses cover only part of what the turn asked, so the conversation moves on with a requirement never enforced — or they require something the turn has no way to produce, so a correct run is held at that turn forever.

How to recognise it in your own task

Two questions per row. Would this let the simulator move on while something the turn asked for is still undone? And can this turn actually produce what the clause demands, with nothing from later turns in hand?

What to do instead

After writing a turn's clauses, re-read that turn's prompt and tick off each ask against a clause. Every ask needs one, each has to be true or false on inspection, and none may name the answer.

Why it costs you

Continuation criteria are what the simulator advances on. A missing clause means every later turn builds on a hole. An unsatisfiable clause is worse: the run cannot proceed at all, on a condition the user never created.

Why it is wrong

3.2Stopping rules that do not gate the turn

Costs pointsMilestones - Continuation Criteria & Assets · Milestones - Milestone Annotations

The mistake

Continuation criteria, read in Leg B to decide when Model B moves on, either miss one of their Leg A turn's asks or demand what that turn cannot produce, such as a later turn's file.

Do this instead

Tick every ask in each Leg A prompt against a clause, and move any clause that needs a later file or ask to the turn that brings it.

Catch it in your own task

Could a response skip one of the Leg A turn's asks and still pass? Could a correct one fail for lack of a later file?

Where the correct guidance is written

Section titles as they print, so you can search for them in the document.

Guidelines · 6.2 Writing the Milestones

Write one clause per requirement of that turn, separated by pipes, each of them true or false on inspection.

That turn is the milestone's Leg A turn.

QC spec · Milestones - Continuation Criteria & Assets

Continuation criteria are present and checkable but under-specified — they would let the simulator advance while a requirement of that turn is still unmet.

In Leg B, the simulator is you.

Why it matters

In Leg B you are the simulator, and continuation criteria are what you advance on: once Model B's response meets them you move to the next milestone, and until it does you send another hint. A missing clause lets Leg B move on with a requirement unmet, and every later turn builds on the hole. A clause the turn cannot satisfy does the opposite: it keeps you hinting Model B toward something the user has not handed over yet.

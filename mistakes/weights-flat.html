All mistakes · Section 4 · A rubric that separates two runs

Weights that do not separate difficulty from lookup

costs points
Rubric Criteria - Overall Rubric Quality
1

What the issue is

Weight 5 and weight 1 handed out without regard to how hard the criterion is: a multi step cross source derivation at the same weight as a direct file read, the same operation at 5 in one row and 3 in the next, or two criteria at weight 5 resting on a single read of one image.

2

Why it matters, and what the project expects

The scale exists to separate discovery from lookup. When it does not, the final score says nothing about whether the model did the hard part, and a run that nails every lookup while missing every derivation still scores well.

Attempter Guidelines5.2 Rubric Weights
Weight is difficulty, never importance. How hard the criterion is to satisfy, or for a negative, how tempting the failure is. A requirement can be critical to the task and still be +1 because meeting it is mechanical.

Importance is the instinct to resist. Price the read, not the stakes.

Attempter Guidelines5.2 Rubric Weights
The four difficulty axes. Tool and source coordination: three or more sources reconciled, or several tools chained. Reasoning depth: multi-step inference, conditional logic, a non trivial calculation. Modality: cross-modal work, image plus structured data plus text. Discovery effort: SQL queries, hidden field lookups, cross document tracing.

Count the axes: three or more is +5, exactly two is +3, one or none is +1.

QC spec appendixRubric Quality Definitions · Incorrect Weights - Major
Criteria that are objectively incorrectly weighted by two levels, e.g., 1 is selected when 5 is appropriate or vice versa. NOTE: Weights should reflect the difficulty of the thing the verifier is testing, NOT importance to responding to the prompt.

Filed under Moderate issues despite its name. The miss it names is two levels: 1 where 5 belongs, or 5 where 1 belongs.

3

How to recognise it in your own task

Sort your criteria by weight and read them in order. The order should match the effort the task actually demands. Count the axes each one requires: tool or source coordination, reasoning depth, modality, discovery. Then read off +5 for three or more, +3 for exactly two, +1 for one or none.

4

Real tasks where this happened

One real task in this project is carrying it. It is evidence that it is real and something to practise spotting it on, not a second version of the rule. Open it and it expands here, with the point on the left and the task's own fields on the right. Escape closes it.

5

What to do instead

Set each weight from its axis count, never from how much the answer matters. Check the ends of the scale first: a direct lookup at 5 is a two level miss, and so is a derivation at 1 that chains sources, media and a calculation. Where two criteria rest on one read, the 5 goes on the reasoning act, not on the restatement.

Weights that do not separate difficulty from lookup

costs points
Rubric Criteria - Incorrect Weights

What the mistake is

Weight 5 and weight 1 handed out without regard to how hard the criterion is: a multi-step cross-source derivation at the same weight as a direct file read, the same operation at 5 in one row and 3 in the next, or two weight-5 criteria resting on a single read of one image.

How to recognise it in your own task

Sort your criteria by weight and read them in order. The order should match the effort the task actually demands. Count the axes each one requires — tool or source coordination, reasoning depth, modality, discovery — and read off +5 for three or more, +3 for exactly two, +1 for one or none.

What to do instead

Price by the difficulty of the read: multi-source derivations and media-only values at 5, direct lookups at 1. A two-level miss is what the taxonomy treats as Major.

Why it costs you

The scale exists to separate discovery from lookup. When it does not, the final score says nothing about whether the model did the hard part, and a run that nails every lookup while missing every derivation still scores well.

What the project says

The rules this breaks, quoted from the documents in Project Resources. Section titles are the ones printed in the document, so you can go and read the whole thing around them.

Attempter Guidelines5.2 Rubric Weights
Weight is difficulty, never importance. How hard the criterion is to satisfy, or for a negative, how tempting the failure is. A requirement can be critical to the task and still be +1 because meeting it is mechanical.

Importance is the instinct to resist. Price the read, not the stakes.

Attempter Guidelines5.2 Rubric Weights
The four difficulty axes. Tool and source coordination: three or more sources reconciled, or several tools chained. Reasoning depth: multi-step inference, conditional logic, a non trivial calculation. Modality: cross-modal work, image plus structured data plus text. Discovery effort: SQL queries, hidden field lookups, cross document tracing.

Count the axes: three or more is +5, exactly two is +3, one or none is +1.

QC spec appendixRubric Quality Definitions · Incorrect Weights - Major
Criteria that are objectively incorrectly weighted by two levels, e.g., 1 is selected when 5 is appropriate or vice versa. NOTE: Weights should reflect the difficulty of the thing the verifier is testing, NOT importance to responding to the prompt.

A two-level miss is Major on its own. One level is a minor issue.

Weight 5 on criterion 12, weight 1 on criterion 17, and one shared weight for any criteria that rest on the same read.

4.3Weights that do not separate difficulty from lookup

Costs pointsRubric Criteria - Overall Rubric Quality

The mistake

Weights that ignore difficulty: a cross source derivation weighted like a direct read, or one operation at 5 in one row and 3 in the next.

Do this instead

Weight by axis count, never importance. A lookup at 5, or a chained derivation at 1, is a two level miss.

Guidelines · 5.2 Rubric Weights

Weight is difficulty, never importance. How hard the criterion is to satisfy, or for a negative, how tempting the failure is.

Priced by four axes (source coordination, reasoning depth, modality, discovery effort): three or more is +5, two is +3, zero or one is +1.

QC spec appendix · Rubric Quality Definitions · Incorrect Weights - Major

Criteria that are objectively incorrectly weighted by two levels, e.g., 1 is selected when 5 is appropriate or vice versa.

A Moderate issue, despite its name.

Why it matters

The scale exists to separate discovery from lookup. When it does not, the final score says nothing about whether the model did the hard part, and a run that nails every lookup while missing every derivation still scores well.

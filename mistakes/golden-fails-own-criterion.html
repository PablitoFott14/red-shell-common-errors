A criterion the golden itself fails

fails the task
34 tasks hit
27 people
Rubric Criteria - Overall Rubric Quality

What the mistake is

A criterion whose expected value or wording is contradicted by the task's own golden, its records or its prompt: a phrase quoted at a different length from the one the golden renders, identical wording demanded across two artifacts that legitimately paraphrase each other, a fact the message threads refute, or a file cited that is not in the inputs archive.

How to recognise it in your own task

Grade the golden against every criterion as the last step before submitting. Separately, read the Desired Outcome clause by clause with the prompts beside it and mark every rule no turn asks for.

What to do instead

Where the golden and a criterion disagree, decide which reading is right and rewrite the other two sources to match. Every rule found only in the outcome is a choice: add it to a prompt, or take it out of the outcome and out of any criterion resting on it. Check every filename a criterion cites against the inputs archive.

Why it costs you

The golden has to pass the complete rubric set. A criterion the golden fails is a broken criterion, not a broken golden, so a correct run gets marked Not Present. Each one counts as Major, and two Majors can fail the task on their own. The commonest cause is a rule that lives only in the Desired Outcome: the agent never sees the outcome, so a criterion resting on it fails a run that did exactly what it was told — and catches your own golden too.

What is wrong
Desired Outcome: "Exactly three records must be listed as unresolved rather than cleared, each with the reason it cannot be closed, and none of the three may be reported as cleared." Criterion 24: "conflict_check.html lists exactly three records as unresolved rather than cleared: job 14-011, invoice 21-018, and the ground covered by job 26-004."
Why it is wrong
Invoice 21-018 is the one invoice in the export with an empty Job column, so the page never returns it: prompt 1 scoped the unresolved records to the jobs the check touches, and 21-018 is reached by neither a party match nor the tract. The golden listed a different set of three and failed both criteria. Nothing the agent was told could have got it there.
What it should have said
Either put the rule in a turn, so the agent is asked for it and can be graded on it, or write the criterion against what the prompts actually request: "conflict_check.html lists job 14-011 and the ground covered by job 26-004 as unresolved rather than cleared."

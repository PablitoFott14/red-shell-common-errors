A criterion the golden itself fails

fails the taskMost common right now
Rubric Criteria - Overall Rubric Quality
1

What the issue is

A criterion that is wrong about the task it grades, so your own golden fails it: a rule only the Desired Outcome states, a phrase quoted at a different length from the one the golden renders, identical wording demanded across two artifacts that legitimately paraphrase each other, a fact the message threads refute, or a file cited that is not in the inputs archive.

2

Why it matters, and what the project expects

The golden has to pass the complete rubric set. A criterion the golden fails is a broken criterion, not a broken golden, so a correct run gets marked Not Present. Each one counts as Major, and Majors on more than 10% of your criteria fail the task outright. The commonest cause is a rule that lives only in the Desired Outcome: the agent never sees the outcome, so a criterion resting on it fails a run that did exactly what it was told, and it catches your own golden too.

Attempter Guidelines1.3 Defining the Draft History
The Draft History (Agent Objective, Desired Outcome, Multimodal Upload) is not embedded in the agent by default. The prompts and multimodal context are the only context the agent has access to, so never assume a requirement is in play just because you wrote it down here. Anything you expect the agent to produce must be explicitly requested through the prompts, turn by turn, or it cannot be graded.

A criterion resting on a rule only the Desired Outcome states fails every run that did what it was asked, your golden included.

3

How to recognise it in your own task

Grade the golden against every criterion as the last step before submitting. Separately, read the Desired Outcome clause by clause with the prompts beside it and mark every rule no turn asks for.

4

Real tasks where this happened

Two real tasks in this project are carrying it. They are evidence that it is real and something to practise spotting it on, not a second version of the rule. Open one and it expands here, with the point on the left and the task's own fields on the right. Escape closes it.

5

What to do instead

Where the golden and a criterion disagree, check both against the prompts and the records. If the criterion is the one they do not support, rewrite it. If the golden is, that is the section 7 error, fixed with a cleanup turn before you complete the conversation. Every rule found only in the outcome is a choice: ask for it in a prompt, without handing over the answer, which means running both legs again, or take it out of the outcome and out of any criterion resting on it. Check every filename a criterion cites against the inputs archive.

Jayden Brown is helping his sister Tamara prepare a memorial photo display for their upcoming family reunion, honoring Daddy and Grandma Pearl while also celebrating the relatives gathering together. Because several original photographs are delicate and…

The issue, in this task
The setup
Prompt 3 asks the agent to put, in the logo beneath the tree, the exact phrase Tamara used when she joked about Terrence's truck. What she says in the records is: That truck is a family heirloom at this point. The golden renders that whole sentence under the tree and its closing message calls it used verbatim.
Where the issue shows up here
In family_photo_tree.html, the logo shows `family heirloom` beneath the tree in place of the original word `FAMILY`.
Why that is wrong
The prompt asks for a sentence, the criterion asks for two words, the golden ships the sentence. A grader has to decide on their own whether a sentence containing the phrase counts as showing the phrase, and on the strict reading the golden fails its own rubric.
What it should have said
In family_photo_tree.html, the lettering beneath the tree in the logo reads `That truck is a family heirloom at this point.`, replacing the original word `FAMILY`.

Abelardo Quintero is a Registered Professional Land Surveyor and the whole of Quintero Land Surveying, PLLC, a one-man practice in Amarillo, Texas, where his wife Rosalba runs the office. In March he was retained as the boundary expert in a Moore County case…

The issue, in this task
The setup
Prompt 1 asks the agent to run the check against every party and the named tract and return "the jobs of mine it touches ... and which ones you cannot honestly clear because the record is too thin". The deliverable indexes records by job number.
Where the issue shows up here
Desired Outcome: "Exactly three records must be listed as unresolved rather than cleared, each with the reason it cannot be closed, and none of the three may be reported as cleared." Criterion 24: "conflict_check.html lists exactly three records as unresolved rather than cleared: job 14-011, invoice 21-018, and the ground covered by job 26-004."
Why that is wrong
Invoice 21-018 is the one invoice in the export with an empty Job column, so the page never returns it: prompt 1 scoped the unresolved records to the jobs the check touches, and 21-018 is reached by neither a party match nor the tract. The golden listed a different set of three and failed both criteria. Nothing the agent was told could have got it there.
What it should have said
Either ask for it in a turn, without stating the count, so the agent can be graded on it, or write the criterion against what the prompts actually request: "conflict_check.html lists job 14-011 and the ground covered by job 26-004 as unresolved rather than cleared."

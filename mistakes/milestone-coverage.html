3.3A Leg A turn no milestone covers, or the same milestone written twice

Costs pointsMilestones - Continuation Criteria & Assets

The mistake

A Leg A turn no milestone covers, usually from a set stopping a turn short, or two milestones on one turn with identical intent and clauses.

Do this instead

Write a milestone for each uncovered turn from its prompt, and delete a duplicate or give it its missing requirement.

Where it happened

2 real tasks. Each shows the spot and what it should have been; click any line to open the task there.

Why it matters

The milestones are the only script you have in Leg B, and every Leg A turn needs at least one. A Leg A turn with none gives you nothing to steer Model B toward or check it against at that point, and gives a replay no script for it. A duplicate checks one condition twice while whatever the second slot was meant to cover goes unrepresented. Where the gap is an off by one, every milestone after it is attached to the wrong Leg A turn.

All mistakes · Section 3 · Milestones

A turn no milestone covers, or the same one written twice

costs points
Milestones - Continuation Criteria & Assets
1

What the issue is

A prompt the task actually ships that no milestone describes, or two milestones on one turn carrying identical intent and identical clauses. The gap usually comes from the set stopping one turn short, or from turn numbering that drifts by one.

2

Why it matters, and what the project expects

The milestones are the script the simulator drives the conversation with, and every turn needs at least one. A turn with none gives the simulator nothing to hold the run to. A duplicate makes it check one condition twice while whatever the second slot was meant to cover goes unrepresented. Where the gap is an off by one, every milestone after it is attached to the wrong turn.

Attempter Guidelines6.2 Writing the Milestones
One milestone per requirement, grouped by turn: A follow up carrying three requirements produces three milestones, not one. A single milestone holding four asks that do not collapse into each other is a paraphrase of the prompt, not an intent spec.

Count requirements, not turns, then check every turn ended up with at least one.

Attempter Guidelines6. Milestones
In the future, the idea is that a user simulator plays every turn after the first and reproduces your intent against runs that go a different way. Until that is wired up and while this is present in the guidelines, you are the simulator, and the milestone set is the only script you have.

A turn with no milestone gives the simulator nothing to hold the run to.

3

How to recognise it in your own task

List the turn index of every milestone beside the prompts. Every prompt number appears at least once, and each milestone's turn is the turn whose prompt asks for what it describes.

4

Real tasks where this happened

Two real tasks in this project are carrying it. They are evidence that it is real and something to practise spotting it on, not a second version of the rule. Open one and it expands here, with the point on the left and the task's own fields on the right. Escape closes it.

5

What to do instead

Count prompts against milestone turns before submitting. Then sort by intent text and look for adjacent duplicates.

Turn 1
Since we delivered Slack Tide to OPB in May, I've rendered the film five times for different places and never ended up writing down which render went where. Three of them has a note next to them on my drive, two do not, and the only record of what those two files are is the file itself, so I pulled them up in MediaInfo on my laptop and grabbed screenshots. Screenshots of the folder with each note open are attached, along with 4 screenshots for two MediaInfo readouts (they didn't fit on one screen). I've also attached the OPB tech spec, the exhibit sent by June in March, and a screenshot of the on-demand intake page which opened earlier this week, as well as the submission requirements for the North Coast Nonfiction Showcase. I need you to whip up `delivery_matrix.html` that contains one row per destination, including what they require, where the requirement came from, and which of my renders meets their requirements. I also need you add if a spec came from a file or from a note. OPB's documents seem to contradict each other, show both and determine which I should follow and tell me why you chose it. The document should allow me to filter by destination and open the source behind each cell. Make sure to end the document by telling me which files I still need to render (video files only, exclude preferences). The co-op is screening the film at the Hollywood on Friday the 25th of September. What they want and our decision are in slack. OPB is already delivered; check my mail to see what went to them and where the sign-off stands, and add that to the corresponding row in the document for me. The film has been locked since May, so you can ignore the co-op's rough-cut notes.
Turn 2
Before I plan the fall I want to be sure the summer deadlines were actually met. Go through the FilmFreeway mails and #festivals, list each deadline that passed, and whether anything went out or got paid; fees would be on the Ink card. Nothing has gone out or been paid since August either. Put OPB's own dates in there too, delivery, acceptance and the invoice. Add it as a section on the page.
Turn 3
June's social team just asked for a vertical for their feeds: 1080x1920, H.264 with AAC, open captions burned in, 60 to 90 seconds from the coast material, by Thursday, October 1. It's going on the schedule. Add it to the page and redo the list.
Turn 4
Put whatever's still on the list onto my Tuesday edit blocks, each before its due date, by updating those calendar entries. Then write June a short note with whatever you'd need her to confirm before I upload to OPB, and send it.
Turn 5
Use her OPB address, from my Vantol Films account.

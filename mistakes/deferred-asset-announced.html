A deferred asset that announces its own turn

fails the task
Input Artifacts - Deferred Asset Handling

What the mistake is

Material available at turn 1 tells the agent what a later turn will ask. Two shapes: a deferred file named for the turn it belongs to, and a turn-1 document carrying the instruction prompt 2 or 3 was meant to deliver.

How to recognise it in your own task

Read every turn-1 input as the agent will. If it tells you what a later turn wants, or what to do when a file arrives, you have it. Then read the deferred filenames cold: price_sheet_for_turn3.pdf fails, sheet_02.pdf is fine.

What to do instead

Cut the forward-looking sentence out of the turn-1 material and put it in the prompt that delivers the file. Name deferred files for what they contain, not for the turn they belong to. And name the file in the sentence that hands it over, so the turn reads like a person attaching a specific document.

Why it costs you

A deferred asset exists to test whether a model can absorb new evidence without a briefing. Told in advance that a file is coming and what to do when it arrives, the model can prepare, and the turn stops measuring anything. Where the advance notice carries the actual instruction rather than a hint of one, the dimension fails outright.

What the project says

The rules this breaks, quoted from the documents in Project Resources. Section titles are the ones printed in the document, so you can go and read the whole thing around them.

Attempter Guidelines6.3 Modifiers
deferred_asset — Material the opening turn did not put in scope arrives or is released into scope at this turn. The file ships only with the later turn where it enters scope, never with the original task attachments (opening turn ones). It should be included in that turn's attachments at upload time.

Nothing in scope at turn 1 may disclose what a later turn will ask for.

Attempter Guidelines1.2.3 Create the Initial Prompt and the Follow-ups
If deferred assets are introduced, point them out: The follow-up must name the asset being introduced so the model knows exactly what new evidence has become available.

Name the file in the turn that hands it over — and only there.

Attempter Guidelines5.2 Rubric Weights
Across turns, acting on material the user has not handed over yet: the file is sitting in the input folder, but nothing in the conversation has asked for it.

This is the failure the deferred asset exists to catch. Announce it early and there is nothing to catch.

Turn 1
I took a call yesterday afternoon from Corwin Steadman offering me the Regional Operations Manager position for the Southeast region at $104,000 base, compared to my current $82,000 base as Operations Manager at HAT2. I jotted down the offer details, the travel expectations, and our current wedding and house savings balances on a notepad (see IMG_4412_call_notes.jpg). The promotion starts late summer, which sits about nine months out from my wedding to Adaeze in May 2027. We know our wedding savings are currently tracking behind our committed costs, and we recently had to throttle our monthly house savings to keep wedding expenses cash-funded. I need an interactive HTML dashboard named dashboard.html that lays out an honest financial comparison between staying at HAT2 versus taking this regional role. Specifically, the dashboard needs to calculate our net take-home cash under both positions after standard income taxes and payroll deductions, project the trajectory of both our wedding fund and house savings fund through our wedding date under both salary tracks, and evaluate how quickly the salary delta would close the wedding funding gap and restore our house savings rate. Please structure this with clear visual KPI cards comparing the gross and net figures alongside a month-by-month trajectory table or chart for both funds through May 2027.
Turn 2
I dug up Jaylen's school calendar for this year and marked it up (see attached IMG_0814_calendar.jpg). My carline mornings are highlighted on it and the custody handovers with Terrance are circled, with the Poplarville details in the margin. Since the regional job runs 60% to 70% travel, about three days a week across the five sites, update dashboard.html with a schedule conflict calendar and a week-by-week matrix across the school year. Map the travel load against what is marked on that calendar, and tell me exactly how many school mornings and how many handovers end up with a travel collision.
Turn 3
Corwin sent over the Southeast region's Q3 travel directives (see attached IMG_0819_travel_policy.jpg). Read it and apply what it actually requires, because it is stricter than what you have modelled and it changes the math. On that schedule we would be out about $200 a month for before-school care, $150 a month for the Poplarville handovers, and $180 a month on meals the company will not cover. Update dashboard.html so those costs come off the raise in the cash comparison, and show me the true adjusted monthly delta and what it does to our savings.
Turn 4
One last thing before I make my final decision. I want dashboard.html to include a dedicated strategic summary section titled "The Cost of the No". Based on all the numbers we have worked through, clearly articulate the trade-off of walking away from this promotion versus taking it, and specify the exact operational and financial conditions that would have to be true for a future regional offer in 2028 to be a sensible "Yes".

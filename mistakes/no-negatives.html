Rubric Criteria - Overall Rubric Quality

What the mistake is

No negative-weight criteria at all, or so few that the traps the scenario was built around go unscored. Every criterion checks something correct is present; nothing checks something wrong is absent. The audited range runs 0% to 17% against the roughly 25% the Guidelines ask for.

How to recognise it in your own task

Count them. Negatives should be about a quarter of the set. Then check the sign is doing what you think: a negative criterion is a failure when it is Present, so if it reads like something a good run does, it is inverted and it will mark down your own golden.

What to do instead

After the Leg A run, read its trajectory for the wrong turns it actually took and write a negative for each one worth catching. State the flaw, never the virtue. Do not invent them to reach a number — but a scenario with no writable negatives usually means the traps are not there.

Why it costs you

A scenario earns its difficulty from the wrong answers sitting next to the right ones: the superseded quote, the decoy invoice, the figure from a different part of the store. Without negatives the rubric cannot see any of them, so a run that walks into every trap and recovers scores the same as one that never went near a trap. They are also the cheapest criteria to write, because Leg A usually shows you which failure to write about.

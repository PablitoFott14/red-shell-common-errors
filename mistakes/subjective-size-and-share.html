All mistakes · Section 6 · The subjective block

Too few subjective criteria, or too much weight on them

costs points
Rubric Criteria - Subjective Block Scope
1

What the issue is

Two numbers, and either one alone is the error. The block carries more than 30% of the task's total weight, or it holds fewer than ten criteria, often five or six, where the Guidelines expect ten or more. The ones present are usually sound and cluster on one property, most often whether the page reads as finished.

2

Why it matters, and what the project expects

The cap keeps the score dominated by deterministic checks. Over it, presentation judgments start to crowd out whether the task was done. Under the count, the block cannot cover a multi panel deliverable: a nested table, a timeline strip or a set of diagrams goes ungraded, and two runs presenting the same correct figures very differently score the same.

Attempter Guidelines8.1 Writing Subjective Rubrics
The expected number of subjective rubrics is 10 or more, but do not optimize for the count itself. The goal is to create a scenario that is complex and complete enough to naturally justify 10+ meaningful subjective criteria, rather than forcing extra rubrics just to hit the number.

Ten is the floor. If the deliverable cannot carry ten, the deliverable is the problem.

Same weight set {-5, -3, -1, +1, +3, +5}; block capped at 30% of total task weight.

Count every weight without its sign on both sides of the division: a subjective −3 adds 3 to the block and 3 to the total.

QC spec appendixMulti-Turn Authoring Standards · Writing Subjective Rubrics
Dimensions worth covering: layout and composition, legibility, visual hierarchy, structure and narrative, cross-artifact consistency, register for the stated audience.

Six dimensions. A block clustered on one of them is short by more than a count.

3

How to recognise it in your own task

Two checks before you submit. Count the rows: ten is the floor, not the target. Then add up the subjective weights without their signs and divide by the same sum over every criterion in the task. Anything over 0.30 is over the cap.

4

Real tasks where this happened

Two real tasks in this project are carrying it. They are evidence that it is real and something to practise spotting it on, not a second version of the rule. Open one and it expands here, with the point on the left and the task's own fields on the right. Escape closes it.

5

What to do instead

Walk the deliverable region by region and write one criterion for whichever region has none. If you are over the cap, first add the objective checks your prompts or milestones call for that no criterion owns, then lower any subjective weight that overstates its impact on the reader. Leave margin rather than landing on 30.0%.

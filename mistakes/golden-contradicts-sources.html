The golden states something its own sources contradict

fails the task
Golden Solution - Artifact Completeness

What the mistake is

A figure, a time, a mapping or a placement written into the golden that the photographed page, the log or the message thread underneath it puts somewhere else. Or a sentence of prose inside the golden stating a total the rest of the same page disagrees with, usually true of an earlier draft. The artifact reads well and every graded column around it is right, which is why it survives to submission.

How to recognise it in your own task

Take every value the golden asserts that no criterion grades and point at the source line it came from. Ungraded values are where this hides, because nothing else in the task checks them. Then search the golden for every sentence containing a total, a count or a recommendation and check it against the tables on the same page.

What to do instead

Correct the golden from the source, in every place the value appears — these travel in threes. A paragraph written at turn 1 is the one most likely to be stale at turn 5.

Why it costs you

The golden is the reference every subjective rating and the pairwise comparison are scored against, so a wrong value in it is graded as correct and a run that reads the source properly is marked down. Where a criterion grades the same detail, the contradiction also makes that criterion unpassable.

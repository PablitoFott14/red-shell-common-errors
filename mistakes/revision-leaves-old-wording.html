All mistakes · Section 7 · Closing the task

A revision that leaves the old wording standing

fails the task
Multi-Turn - Revision Turn Handling
1

What the issue is

The revision turn changes the thing it was asked to change and leaves a sentence, a label or a comment that depended on it untouched, so the final artifact states the old position and the new one at the same time.

2

Why it matters, and what the project expects

Revision Turn Handling fails on a regression, and this is the commonest form: the graded value moves, the prose around it does not. A reader meets an artifact arguing with itself, and a grader checking your golden against a criterion can land on the stale half and mark it Not Present.

QC specMulti-Turn - Revision Turn Handling
A correct golden shows the revision applied to work that already existed: it satisfies the revised brief while still carrying what the earlier turns established. FAIL: both present but contradictory: March rows gone and the flags survived, but the total at the bottom still includes the March amounts. Or the header still says “5 receipts” when 3 remain. The edit touched the table and not the parts that depend on it.

The spec names this exact shape as a failing case.

Attempter Guidelines6.3 Modifiers
The golden has to show the edit applied to work that already existed: earlier content kept, the change visible, nothing left stale that depended on it. A total that still includes removed rows, or a header still saying five when three remain, fails.

The rule attached to the revision modifier. “Nothing left stale that depended on it” is the whole test.

3

How to recognise it in your own task

After every revision turn, search the artifact for the value, the label and the name that moved, and read each hit in place.

4

Real tasks where this happened

One real task in this project is carrying it. It is evidence that it is real and something to practise spotting it on, not a second version of the rule. Open it and it expands here, with the point on the left and the task's own fields on the right. Escape closes it.

5

What to do instead

A revision is finished when nothing left on the page describes the state before it. If a stale sentence survives the revision turn, send a cleanup turn in the user's voice that points at the area (“the card at the top still counts the old list”), never at the replacement wording.

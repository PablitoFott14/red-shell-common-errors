All mistakes · Section 7 · Closing the task

A revision that leaves the old wording standing

fails the task
Multi-Turn - Revision Turn Handling

What the mistake is

The revision turn changes the thing it was asked to change and leaves every sentence, label and comment that depended on it untouched, so the final artifact states the old position and the new one at the same time.

How to recognise it in your own task

After every revision turn, search the artifact for the value, the label and the name that moved, and read each hit in place.

What to do instead

A revision is finished when nothing left on the page describes the state before it. Change one thing precisely, then re-read the whole artifact for every sentence that just became false.

Why it costs you

Revision Turn Handling fails on a regression, and this is the commonest form: the graded value moves, the prose around it does not. A reader meets an artifact arguing with itself, and a grader reading a criterion against the stale half marks a correct run Not Present.

What the project says

The rules this breaks, quoted from the documents in Project Resources. Section titles are the ones printed in the document, so you can go and read the whole thing around them.

A correct golden shows the revision applied to work that already existed: it satisfies the revised brief while still carrying what the earlier turns established. FAIL: both present but contradictory — March rows gone and the flags survived, but the total at the bottom still includes the March amounts. Or the header still says “5 receipts” when 3 remain. The edit touched the table and not the parts that depend on it.

The spec names this exact shape as a failing case.

Attempter Guidelines6.3 Modifiers
The golden has to show the edit applied to work that already existed: earlier content kept, the change visible, nothing left stale that depended on it. A total that still includes removed rows, or a header still saying five when three remain, fails.

The rule attached to the revision modifier. “Nothing left stale that depended on it” is the whole test.

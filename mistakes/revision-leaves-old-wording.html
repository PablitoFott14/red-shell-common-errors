7.2A revision that leaves the old wording standing

Fails the taskMulti-Turn - Revision Turn Handling

The mistake

A revision changes what was asked but leaves wording that depended on it, so the artifact states the old position and the new one.

Do this instead

Send Model B a Leg B cleanup turn that points at the stale area, never at the new wording.

Guidelines · 6.3 Modifiers

The golden has to show the edit applied to work that already existed: earlier content kept, the change visible, nothing left stale that depended on it.
Why it matters

Revision Turn Handling fails on a regression, and this is the commonest form: the graded value moves, the prose around it does not. A reader meets an artifact arguing with itself, and a grader checking your golden against a criterion can land on the stale half and mark it Not Present.

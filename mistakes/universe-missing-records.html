The rules point at records the universe does not hold

fails the task
Trajectory - Feasibility With Tools
1

What the issue is

The scenario's rules read off a catalog, a policy page, a price list or a subscription that the assigned universe does not contain. Nothing in the folder shows it: the prompts, the rules file and the criteria all read as though the data is there.

2

Why it matters, and what the project expects

A rule pointing at data no tool returns cannot be followed by any run, so every criterion downstream of it is unreachable. Not wrong. Impossible. It takes the difficulty with it too: where the second system is what made the task multi system, the task ships with one side of every decision resolving to nothing.

Attempter Guidelines1.2.1 Start With the Universe
Confirm there is enough material. The universe combined with the multimodal input has to sustain the opening prompt and every follow-up you intend to add, not just the first request.

Every rule you write has to have a record behind it, for every turn, not only turn 1.

Attempter Guidelines4. Valid vs. Invalid Model Failures
Data that is not in the server, asked for anyway, and the model penalised for not finding it.

Listed under “Not valid: your defect, or the platform's”. It grades your defect, not the model's.

QC specTrajectory - Feasibility With Tools
The primary request is impractical or impossible and can't be answered by the tools available or enabled for the task.

That is the option QC picks at score 2. Unreachable is worse than hard: no run can satisfy it.

3

How to recognise it in your own task

The tell is in your own golden: a search that comes back empty, a steering turn telling the agent to reach the page another way, and a deliverable that flags its own figures as unconfirmed.

4

Real tasks where this happened

One real task in this project is carrying it. They are evidence that it is real and something to practise spotting it on, not a second version of the rule. Open one and it expands here, with the point on the left and the task's own fields on the right. Escape closes it.

5

What to do instead

At scenario design time, open the universe and confirm it returns every record your rules point at, one query per rule. An empty search on the first golden turn is a signal to fix the source, never something to steer around.

The issue, in this task
The setup
Five items on a neighbour's board have to be priced against what Amazon Fresh actually sells, with the delivery fee taken from Fresh's policy and one apartment's quantity from a Subscribe and Save record. The task is assigned to openclaw_mm_thaddeus_okonjo_commerce_product.
Where the issue shows up here
Rule 1 prices every line "at the pack option we add to the cart from Fresh's catalog"; rule 5 takes the delivery fee from "Fresh's Delivery Policy and nothing else"; rule 7 allows only claims "Fresh's own catalog or policies back up". That universe's Fresh catalog holds 39 products and none of the five board items, its policies and faqs lists are both empty, and it carries no subscription records at all.
Why that is wrong
The golden hit the wall in the run itself. Turn 3 tells the agent "all five items are sold on fresh, so if the search is coming back empty try getting to the product pages and the policy pages another way", turn 7 becomes "leave anything you genuinely cant confirm as is", and the delivered page states "its catalog search returns nothing, so I can't pull the building's real prices" - so every price on it is the board figure flagged unconfirmed, and the criteria wanting $0.55 a lb, $5.45 a tub and a $4.99 fee cannot be reached by anyone.
What it should have said
Before writing the rules, call the tools against the assigned universe: search the catalog for each item you intend to price, and open the policy and subscription records you intend to cite. Where they are not there, change the universe rather than the rules - the arithmetic in this task was exact and every other part of it held up.
